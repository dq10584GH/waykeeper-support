## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I track my supply inventory?
Go to the **Supplies** tab, tap **+** to add items, and include details like quantity and expiration date. The app will alert you when items are running low or expiring.

### Can I use the map without internet?
Yes! Go to the **Map** tab, tap the download icon to save a region for offline use. You can also add custom markers for water sources, shelters, and other key locations.

### How do checklists work?
The **Checklists** tab has pre-built emergency checklists. Check off items as you go, and export any list as a PDF to share or print.

### How does the quiz work?
Head to the **Skills** tab or Home screen to start a quiz. Answer timed questions across survival categories and track your best scores.

### Is my data backed up?
If you have iCloud enabled, Waykeeper can sync your data across devices via **iCloud/CloudKit**. Check **Settings** to manage sync preferences.

### How do I reset my progress?
Go to **Settings** and look for reset options. You can reset streaks, quiz scores, or skill progress individually.

### Do you collect my personal data?
No. Waykeeper works entirely on your device. We don't collect, store, or share personal data. See our [Privacy Policy](https://dq10584gh.github.io/waykeeper-privacy/) for full details.
